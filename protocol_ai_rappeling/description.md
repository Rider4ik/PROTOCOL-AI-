# PROTOCOL AI AUTONOMOUS RAPPELLING

**PROTOCOL AI AUTONOMOUS RAPPELLING** introduces an autonomous helicopter insertion system designed to make AI deployments more dynamic, deliberate, and authentic.

When enemy forces are detected, helicopters can approach the operational area and deploy infantry using fast-rope insertion. Instead of relying exclusively on conventional landings, AI units can descend directly from hovering helicopters, inserting troops onto rooftops, into buildings, or onto open terrain.

## FEATURES

- **Autonomous Fast-Rope Insertion** – When enemy forces are detected, the helicopter approaches the area and deploys its troops using ropes, allowing for a dynamic response to developing combat situations.
- **Deployment Groups** – When infantry boards a helicopter, a new group is formed for the subsequent rope insertion, allowing embarked personnel to be organized into a dedicated deployment force.
- **Rooftop and Building Insertion** – Units can descend by rope onto rooftops and into buildings, enabling specialized insertions into urban environments and other elevated positions.
- **Open-Terrain Insertion** – Infantry can also be deployed by rope onto open ground, allowing helicopters to insert troops without requiring a conventional landing.

## COMPATIBILITY

Works with any mods.
