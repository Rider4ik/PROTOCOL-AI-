# PROTOCOL AI JAMMING

**PROTOCOL AI JAMMING** introduces electronic warfare and jamming mechanics to Arma 3, enhancing battlefield realism through configurable jamming systems designed to disrupt enemy communications and drone operations.

# HOW IT WORKS

When a unit equipped with a radar-type configuration is placed on the battlefield, it acts as an electronic warfare jammer.

When an enemy unit enters the configured jamming radius, the system activates and disrupts supported signals and communications, accompanied by immersive electronic interference sound effects.

If the radar unit is destroyed, the jamming system is automatically deactivated.

# FEATURES

- **Drone Jamming** – Disrupts enemy drone operations within the jammer's effective range.
- **Chat Jamming** – Disrupts supported in-game text chat communications.
- **Radio Jamming** – Disrupts supported in-game radio communications between AI units and players.
- **Voice Chat Jamming** – Disrupts supported voice communication systems.
- **ACRE2 Integration** – Provides jamming support for the ACRE2 radio communication system.
- **Task Force Radio (TFAR) Integration** – Provides jamming support for TFAR radio communications.
- **Audio Interference Effects** – Electronic interference sound effects provide immersive feedback while jamming is active.
- **Configurable Jamming Radius** – Allows you to customize the effective range of the jamming system to suit your mission requirements and gameplay balance.

# COMPATIBILITY

Compatible with **CUP**, **RHS**, and other mods.
