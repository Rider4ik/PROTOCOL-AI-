# PROTOCOL AI NAVY SEAL

**PROTOCOL AI NAVY SEAL** enhances AI amphibious operations in Arma 3, introducing realistic boat-based landings and combat behavior inspired by US Navy SEAL operational doctrine.

The mod allows AI units to conduct coordinated amphibious assaults, approach the shoreline, disembark in shallow water, and continue their attack using tactical movement, flanking, maneuvering, and realistic combat behavior.

## FEATURES

1. **Combat Boat Deployment** – AI units can deploy from boats during combat operations.
2. **Automatic Shoreline Approach** – When enemy forces are detected, boats can move toward the shoreline.
3. **Coastal Navigation** – Boats approach suitable coastal areas instead of stopping randomly offshore.
4. **Shallow-Water Disembarkation** – Infantry disembarks in shallow water close to the beach.
5. **Post-Landing Operations** – After landing, units leave the water and continue their operation on land.

## COMPATIBILITY

Works with any mods.
