# ANTI-AIR IS EFFECTIVE NOW

**PROTOCOL AI ANTI-AIR: RELOCATE AND ANTI-MISSILE** adds advanced tactical behavior for wheeled anti-aircraft vehicles in Arma 3.

The system makes mobile air-defense units more dynamic and tactical by allowing them to react to battlefield conditions instead of remaining in the same position throughout combat.

# FEATURES

- **Tactical Relocation** – After engaging targets, anti-air units can relocate to new positions to reduce the risk of enemy counterattacks and avoid remaining exposed.
- **Smoke During Relocation** – Anti-air vehicles deploy smoke while relocating, providing additional concealment during movement.
- **Anti-Missile Defense** – Anti-air units can attempt to detect and engage guided missiles, adding an additional layer of protection against incoming missile threats.
- **Dynamic Combat Behavior** – Anti-air vehicles are no longer limited to static defensive positions and can actively adapt their behavior during combat.
- **Mobile Air Defense** – Designed specifically to give wheeled anti-air platforms a more realistic and tactical battlefield role.

# COMPATIBILITY

Compatible with other mods.
