# Protocol AI

**Protocol AI** is a tactical AI enhancement mod for Arma 3 focused on bringing realistic military tactics, combat doctrines, and battlefield behavior to the game.

The goal is to make AI units behave more intelligently and dynamically, adapting their decisions, formations, and actions to the combat situation, surrounding environment, and assigned tactical doctrine.

The mod introduces a variety of infantry tactics, coordinated maneuvers, realistic suppression, dynamic cover usage, vehicle and aircraft behavior, and battlefield responses to emerging threats.

# INFANTRY SYSTEMS

## Environmental Tactics

AI infantry adapts its behavior to different combat environments:

- **CITY** – Urban combat tactics and movement through built-up areas.

  **CONTAINS:** [PROTOCOL AI CITY TACTICS](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3803702313)

- **FOREST** – Tactical movement and combat behavior in wooded terrain.

- **CQB** – Close-quarters combat tactics for buildings and confined spaces.

  **CONTAINS:** [PROTOCOL AI CQB](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3799391254)

## Combat Doctrines

AI groups can employ a variety of real-world-inspired military doctrines depending on the battlefield situation:

- **BOUNDING OVERWATCH** – Coordinated movement with alternating covering and advancing elements.
- **PAIRS** – Two-man tactical coordination.

  **CONTAINS:** [PROTOCOL AI COMBAT PAIRS](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3802121807)

- **TRIPLET** – Three-man tactical coordination.
- **FIRE AND MANEUVER** – One element provides suppressive fire while another maneuvers.
- **FLANKING** – Attempts to outmaneuver enemy positions from the sides.
- **ENCIRCLEMENT** – Attempts to surround enemy forces.
- **RECONNAISSANCE BY FIRE** – Uses controlled fire to reveal or engage suspected enemy positions.
- **CENTRAL PEEL** – Tactical disengagement under fire while maintaining covering fire.
- **ALL-ROUND DEFENSE** – Establishes defensive positions covering multiple directions.
- **AMBUSH** – Uses positioning and surprise to engage enemy forces.

## Advanced Infantry Combat Behavior

- **Corner Usage** – Tactical use of corners and angles when engaging enemies.
- **Dynamic Cover System** – AI dynamically searches for and uses available cover.
- **Realistic Suppression** – Units react to incoming fire and suppressive pressure.
- **Distance-Based Detection** – Enemy detection behavior takes engagement distance into account.
- **Blind Fire** – Units can fire from behind cover without fully exposing themselves.
- **Dynamic Formations** – Combat formations change depending on the situation, terrain, and enemy pressure.
- **Doctrine-Based Stances** – Units dynamically use standing, crouching, and prone positions according to their tactical role and combat conditions.
- **Aggressive Grenade Usage** – AI makes tactical use of hand grenades.
- **Underbarrel Grenade Launchers** – Units equipped with grenade launchers can use them aggressively for suppression and engagement.

# FPV DRONE REACTION

Infantry units can react to incoming FPV drones:

- Units without suitable weapons attempt to evade incoming FPV drones.
- Armed units can engage and attempt to destroy FPV drones according to their capabilities and tactical behavior.
