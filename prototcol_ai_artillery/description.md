# PROTOCOL AI ARTILLERY: RELOCATE & COUNTER-BATTERY

**PROTOCOL AI ARTILLERY: RELOCATE & COUNTER-BATTERY** is a realistic artillery system designed to make AI artillery behave more dynamically and authentically on the battlefield.

Artillery units no longer simply remain in one position and continuously fire at targets. After completing fire missions, artillery units can relocate to new random positions, making them harder to detect and destroy.

The system also introduces counter-battery warfare, allowing opposing artillery units to detect and engage each other. This creates an evolving artillery battle where batteries must constantly balance firepower, survivability, and repositioning.

## FEATURES

- **Fire on Detected Targets** – Artillery units can engage enemy forces after they are detected, allowing AI batteries to react dynamically to battlefield contacts.
- **Artillery Relocation** – After firing, artillery units can move to new random positions instead of remaining in the same location.
- **Counter-Battery Fire** – Enemy artillery units can engage opposing artillery, creating realistic artillery-versus-artillery combat.
- **Random Number of Shots** – Enable a randomized number of shots to make artillery behavior less predictable.
- **Configurable Number of Shots** – Set the exact number of shots an artillery unit can fire before relocating.

## COMPATIBILITY

Works with any mods.
