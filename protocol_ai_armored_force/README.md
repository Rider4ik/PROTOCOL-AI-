# PROTOCOL AI ARMORED FORCE

**PROTOCOL AI ARMORED FORCE** is an AI enhancement mod for Arma 3 designed to improve the behavior of ground combat vehicles and bring their battlefield actions closer to realistic military tactics, armored warfare doctrines, and battlefield decision-making.

The mod aims to make AI-controlled armored vehicles behave more intelligently in combat by using their weapons effectively, taking advantage of available cover, and adapting their actions to changing battlefield conditions.

# FEATURES

- **ATGM Usage** – Improves the tactical use of anti-tank guided missiles (ATGMs), allowing AI vehicles equipped with them to employ these weapons against appropriate targets.
- **Smoke Screen Deployment** – Enables AI crews to use smoke launchers to conceal their vehicles, disrupt enemy observation, and support repositioning or withdrawal under fire.
- **Use of Cover** – Encourages armored vehicles to take advantage of available terrain and protective positions instead of remaining unnecessarily exposed to enemy fire.
- **Engagement Range and Combat Radius** – Improves vehicle engagement behavior by taking combat distance and engagement range into account, helping AI vehicles fight at more appropriate tactical distances.
- **Main Gun Employment** – Enhances the tactical use of the main cannon, encouraging AI crews to engage suitable targets with their primary weapon according to battlefield conditions.

# COMPATIBILITY

Compatible with other mods.
