# PROTOCOL AI AIRBORNE

**PROTOCOL AI AIRBORNE** is a configurable airborne deployment system designed to bring automatic and dynamic parachute insertions to Arma 3.

The system allows AI units to deploy by parachute from both helicopters and aircraft. Airborne deployments are triggered automatically when enemy forces are detected within the configured area, allowing AI forces to conduct airborne insertions without constant player control.

The system is highly configurable, giving you control over how and when airborne troops are deployed.

## FEATURES

- **Helicopter Airborne Deployment** – AI units can parachute from helicopters.
- **Aircraft Airborne Deployment** – AI units can parachute from transport aircraft.
- **Configurable Flight Altitude** – Set the desired deployment altitude for helicopters and aircraft.
- **Configurable Parachute Interval** – Control the time interval between individual parachute deployments.
- **Configurable Deployment Distance** – Determine how close to or how far from enemy forces AI units will be deployed.

## COMPATIBILITY

Works with any mods.
