# PROTOCOL AI AIRCRAFT

**PROTOCOL AI AIRCRAFT** improves aircraft AI behavior in Arma 3, making aerial combat more realistic, dynamic, and immersive.

The mod enhances the behavior of helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, introducing more tactical reactions and realistic combat actions. Aircraft can engage enemy targets, employ their weapons, and use defensive countermeasures depending on the situation.

# FEATURES

- **Helicopter AI Behavior** – Improved helicopter combat behavior, tactical maneuvering, and engagement reactions.
- **Fixed-Wing Aircraft AI** – More dynamic behavior for fighter jets, attack aircraft, and other fixed-wing aircraft during combat.
- **Rocket Launching** – AI aircraft can use rockets against enemy ground and aerial targets when appropriate.
- **Bomb Deployment** – AI aircraft can deploy bombs against enemy positions and ground targets.
- **Countermeasures Deployment** – Aircraft can release defensive flares in response to incoming heat-seeking missiles.

# COMPATIBILITY

Compatible with other mods.
